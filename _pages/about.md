Hi, I am Ali!
------

<p align="justify">
I am a graduate student in Mechatronics &amp; Robotics at the Technical University of Munich (TUM), after my BSc in Mechanical Engineering at Boğaziçi University in Istanbul.
</p>

<p align="justify">
I am interested in dexterous manipulation, reinforcement learning, and generative models. I have worked with various robots, including the iiwa, Franka, UR10, drones, and Super Megabot.
</p>

<p align="justify">
In the <A HREF="/projects/">Projects</A> section, you can find summaries of the projects I was involved in.
</p>

---

<p align="justify">
During my leisure moments, I enjoy participating in a wide range of sports, including football, calisthenics, and bouldering. From the earliest stages of self-discovery, I have been drawn to making melodies and writing songs. I currently have a band "Eser Miktarda" where I contribute as the guitarist and lead vocalist. I was also a tenor in Bogazici University Jazz Choir for 3 years. Furthermore, I enjoy creating realistic drawings using charcoal.
</p>
